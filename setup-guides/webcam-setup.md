# Webcam Setup

## Purpose

Provides instructions for connecting, configuring, and testing a webcam used for video conferencing.

## Setup Steps

### 1. Connect the Webcam

Connect the webcam to the computer or video conferencing system using the appropriate USB connection.

### 2. Position the Webcam

Place the webcam in a positon that provides a clear view of the meeting participants.

Addjust the camera angle as needed.

### 3. Verify Device Detection

Open the computer's device settings and confirm that the webcam is recognized.

### 4. Configure the Video Application

Open the video conferencing application.

Select the webcam as the camera device.

### 5. Test the camera

Start with application's camera preview and verify:

- Video is displayed
- Image is clear
- Camera angle is correct
- Lighting is adequate

### 6. Test During a Meeting

Join a test meeting and comfirm that other participants can see the video correctly.

## Troubleshooting

If the webcam does not work, check:

- USB connection
- Camera privacy settings
- Device detection
- Camera selection in the application
- Camera drivers
- Whether another application is using the camera

## Final Verification

Confirm that the webcam is recognized, selected in the video conferencing application, and providing a clear video image.
