# No Audio

## Problem

No sound can be heard from the speaker during a video conference.

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Check Speaker Connection

Verify that the speakers are securely connected to the computer or vide conferencing system.

### 2. Check Volume

Confirm that the system volume and application volume are turned up.

### 3. Check Mute Status

Verify that the speakers or audio output are not muted.

### 4. Check Output Device

Open the computer's sound settings am confirm that the correct speakers are selected.

### 5. Check Application Settings

Open the video conferencing application's audio settings and confirm that the correct speaker device is selected.

### 6. Test System Audio

Play a system sound or other audio to determine whether the issue affects the entire computer or only the conferencing application.

### 7. Restart the Application

Close and reopen the video conferencing application and test the audio again.

## Expected Result

Audio should play clearly through the selected conference room speakers.

## Escalation

If audio remains unavailable after troubleshooting, document the steps performed and escalate the issue for further technical support.
