# Camera Not Working

## Problem

The video confeencing camera is not displaying video or is not being detected by the computer or conferencing application.

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Check the Connection

Verify that the camera is securely connected to the computer or video conferencing system.

If using USB, disconnec and reconnect the cable.

### 2. Check Camera Power

Confirm that the camera is receiving power and that any required indicator light is active.

### 3. Check Device Detection

Open the computer's device settings and verify that the camera is recognized.

### 4. Check Camera Permissions

Verify that the video conferencing application has permisson to access the camera.

### 5. Check Application Settings

Open the application's video settings and confirm that the correct camera is selected.

### 6. Close Other Applications

Close other applications that may be using the camera.

Then reopen the video conferencing application and test again.

### 7. Restart the Device

If the camera is still not working, restart the computer or conferencing system and test the camera again.

## Expected Result

The camera should be detected by the system and display a clear video image in the conferencing appplication.

## Escalation

If the camera remains unavailable after troubleshooting, document the steps performed and escalate the issue for further hardware or technical support.
