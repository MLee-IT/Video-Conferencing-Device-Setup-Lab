# Microphone Not Working

## Problem

The microphone is not detecting audio or other meeting participants cannot hear the user.

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Check the Connection

Verify that the microphone is securely connected to the computer or video conferencing system.

If using USB, disconnect and reconnect the cable.

### 2. Check Mute Status

Confirm that the microphone is not muted on the device, computer, or video conferencing application.

### 3. Check Device Detection

Open the computer's sound settings and verify that the microphone is recognized.

### 4. Check Microphone Permissions

Verify that the video conferencing application has permission to access the microphone.

### 5. Check Input Device

Open the application's audio settings and confirm that the correct microphone is selected.

### 6. Test the Microphone

Speak into the mircophone and verify that the input level responds.

### 7. Restart the Application

Close and reopen the video conferencing application, then test the microphone again.

## Expected Result

The microphone should be detected and provide clear audio to other meeting participants.

## Escalation

If the mirophone remains unavailable after troubleshooting, document the steps performed and escalate the issue for further technical support.
